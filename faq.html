BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good questions. Straight answers.

Here is what the learning platform does, what it does not do and how to get the best from it.

Do I need previous digital marketing experience?

No. The learning path begins with foundations. New terms are explained in plain language and examples build gradually.

Are all ten modules available?

Yes. Module 01 has ten lessons. Modules 02–10 each include guided lessons, checkpoints, a module quiz, a practical project worksheet and a glossary.

Does my progress save?

Lesson completion and project drafts are saved in local browser storage on the device you use. The information is not synced to an account or server. Browser cleanup, private browsing or changing devices may affect it, so download project notes you want to keep.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes. The layouts are designed to adapt to small screens. Keep your browser updated and use the download controls when you want a copy of your work.

Will completing the course guarantee a job or income?

No. Learning can help build understanding and practice work, but hiring and income depend on many factors such as skill, experience, portfolio quality, market needs and communication. No outcome is guaranteed.

Will I receive an official certificate?

This static learning site does not currently issue verified certificates. Some external platforms provide their own courses or credentials; check their official terms and requirements.

Are Maple Lane’s results real?

No. Maple Lane Home Bakery is fictional. Any sample orders, budgets, revenue or conversion rates are illustrative and must not be presented as real client results.

Can I use practice projects in my portfolio?

Yes, as practice projects. Clearly label them as fictional or self-initiated work. Do not imply that a real client paid for the work, and do not invent testimonials or performance outcomes.

Does the community or AI assistant work?

Not yet. A live community needs accounts, a database, moderation and privacy controls. An AI assistant needs a secure backend. Neither is enabled in this static site, and we do not expose private API keys in the browser.

Why are some external links included?

They point to official or primary learning resources. Platform interfaces, policies and course catalogues can change, so check the source itself before using it in a live campaign.

What should I do if something does not work?

Refresh the page, check that JavaScript is enabled and try a current browser. If a progress draft matters, download a backup from the module worksheet or workbook before clearing site data.
