CHANGING PLATFORMS · VERIFIED SOURCES

Updates without the guesswork.

Digital platforms change their interfaces, products and policies. We prefer to explain how updates are verified rather than publish undated rumours or screenshots as facts.

How an update should be published

  1. Publication date: when the learning note was written or checked.
  2. Event date: when the platform change actually happened, when known.
  3. Primary source: an official product note, policy or help document.
  4. What changed: a short explanation in plain language.
  5. Why it matters: what a learner or small business should review.
  6. What remains uncertain: regions, account types, rollout timing or testing limits.
